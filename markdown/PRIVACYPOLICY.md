## 1. Introduction

EasyHire VA Solutions ("we," "us," "the Platform") is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we protect it.

## 2. What Data We Collect

### From Job Seekers

- Name, email, phone number, location
- Resume/CV files
- Work history, skills, desired salary range
- Application history and status

### From Employers

- Company name, description, website, industry
- Contact person's name and email
- Billing information (processed by our payment provider, not stored by us directly)

### Automatically Collected

- Login timestamps, IP address (for security/fraud prevention)
- Basic usage analytics (pages visited, actions taken)

## 3. Why We Collect This Data

We process your personal data to:

- Create and manage your account
- Match Job Seekers with relevant job postings
- Allow Employers to review applications
- Send transactional emails (application confirmations, status updates, job alerts)
- Process payments for Employer subscriptions/job postings
- Prevent fraud and enforce our Terms of Service
- Improve the Platform

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under RA 10173, we process your data based on:

- **Consent** — when you register and agree to this policy
- **Contract necessity** — to provide the service you signed up for
- **Legitimate interest** — fraud prevention, platform security

## 5. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

| Third Party                 | Purpose                                                                                     |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Supabase                    | Database & file storage hosting                                                             |
| Resend                      | Sending transactional emails                                                                |
| Stripe / PayMongo           | Payment processing (Employers only)                                                         |
| Employers (for Job Seekers) | Only when you apply to a job, your profile/resume becomes visible to that specific employer |

Each of these providers has its own privacy and security practices; we select providers that maintain reasonable data protection standards.

## 6. Job Seeker Visibility Controls

Job Seekers can control whether their profile is visible to Employers browsing candidates, separate from actively applying to specific jobs. Your resume and contact details are only shared with an Employer when you either (a) apply to their posting, or (b) opt into visibility and they view your profile.

## 8. Your Rights Under the Data Privacy Act

As a data subject under RA 10173, you have the right to:

- **Be informed** that your data is being collected and processed
- **Access** your personal data held by us
- **Correct** inaccurate data
- **Object** to processing of your data
- **Data portability** — request a copy of your data in a portable format
- **Erasure/blocking** — request deletion of your data, subject to legal exceptions
- **File a complaint** with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) if you believe your rights have been violated
